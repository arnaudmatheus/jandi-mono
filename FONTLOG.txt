FONTLOG for Jandí Mono
======================

Basic font information
----------------------
Jandí Mono is a monospaced font for terminals and desktop interfaces, made for
the Jandí theme for Omarchy. It is a derivative of Monaspace Radon: Radon's
cursive letters are redrawn with a simulated Renaissance broad-nib pen held at
one fixed angle, while Radon's grid, metrics, symbols, box drawing, braille and
OpenType features are kept.

Two families are built from the same sources:
- Jandi Mono      Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic
- Jandi Mono NF   the same, plus Nerd Fonts icons

Released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt). Following the
OFL, it does not use the Reserved Font Names of the original ("Monaspace",
"Argon", "Neon", "Xenon", "Radon", "Krypton").

ChangeLog
---------
0.1.0 (2026-10-16)
- Redrawn from centreline skeletons swept by a rectangular nib at 35°, slant
  7° (Regular/Bold) and 13° (Italic/Bold Italic): a–z, A–Z, 0–9, dotless i/j,
  ß æ ø œ ł đ ħ ĸ ŋ þ ð Æ Ø Œ Ł Đ Ð Ħ Ŋ Þ, every precomposed accented Latin
  letter in Radon's character set, 17 combining marks and their .case and
  Vietnamese stacked forms.
- Added GPOS mark attachment and corrected the GDEF class of the combining
  marks (Radon has no GPOS and files its marks as components), so sequences
  such as g + U+0303 (Guarani g̃) and G + U+0303 position correctly.
- Regenerated Radon's texture-healing variants (.left/.right/.both) from the
  same skeletons, so its contextual alternates keep working.
- Distinct confusables: flagged and footed 1, curved l, serifed I, slashed 0.
- Czech/Slovak carons on ď ľ ť Ľ drawn as apostrophes.
- NF builds: 10,828 Nerd Fonts icon glyphs copied from MonaspiceRn Nerd Font
  (Nerd Fonts' patch of Radon, already scaled to this cell).

Still from Radon in 0.1.0: punctuation and symbols, zero.alt01–04, i.alt and
l.alt, A.italic/B.italic, numerator/denominator/superior/inferior figures.
